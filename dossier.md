## 4. Weather: How Conditions Evolved

### Qualifying — Saturday 6 July (session key 9554)
A cold, damp-adjacent but ultimately **dry** hour of running:

- **Air temperature:** ~12.4 °C at the start, rising gradually to peak around **14.3 °C** mid-session, easing back to ~13.6 °C late on.
- **Track temperature:** started at just **19.4 °C**, climbed to a session peak of **~24 °C** (14:12–14:13 UTC), then fell back to ~20 °C as the session closed.
- **Humidity:** high throughout — **~83–85%** early, dipping to a low of about **70–72%** mid-session.
- **Rainfall:** **0** at every reading — no measurable rain during qualifying.
- **Pressure:** steady around **986.6–987.4 hPa**, drifting slightly upward through the hour.
- **Wind:** very light, mostly 1–2 m/s (peaking ~4.4 m/s once at 14:20 UTC), direction swinging from south-westerly to north-westerly.

### Race — Sunday 7 July (session key 9558)
A classic changeable Silverstone afternoon, and the story of the race:

- **Air temperature:** warmer than Saturday, sitting around **15.9 °C** at the start and hovering between ~15.3 °C and ~16.4 °C.
- **Track temperature:** began at **28.1 °C** — but this is where it gets interesting: the first flurries of **rainfall=1** appear in the readings from **13:10 UTC** through about **13:21 UTC**, and again briefly around **13:26–13:27 UTC**. Those first ~15 minutes of the race window carried light rain as a shower passed over the circuit.
- **Track temperature swings:** as the rain arrived the track dropped from **31.3 °C** to **26.5 °C**, then surged dramatically once the rain stopped — climbing to **35.2 °C**, peaking at **37.1 °C** (13:30 UTC) before settling in the mid-30s (~35 °C) for the bulk of the race.
- **Humidity:** lower than Saturday, **62–75%**, rising during the shower.
- **Pressure:** higher and steadier than Saturday, **~991.7–992.0 hPa**.
- **Wind:** light throughout, mostly under 2.2 m/s.

**The story in one line:** qualifying was run on a cold, dry, grey afternoon (~21–24 °C track), while the race started under a brief passing shower that dropped the track temperature and then gave way to a much warmer, drying, faster track that climbed into the mid-30s — the crossover that triggered the field's tyre strategy calls and set up Hamilton's charge.

*Interpreting the pattern:* Hamilton ran second behind Russell in the opening stint, holding steady low-1:31s. As the rain passed and the track came back to him, he hit the front — readings show him P1 from lap 18. There was a turbulent mid-race window during the shower's aftermath and the first pit cycle (laps 18–30 include in-lap/out-lap effects and track-position changes: P3 on laps 20–26, then back to P1/P2), before he took firm control. His race lap progression: steady 1:31s opening stint → weather/crossover laps in the 1:33–1:40s+ (with the 2:00.866 on lap 28 reflecting a heavy rain-affected lap) → and then low 1:29s on fresh rubber in the closing stint, culminating in the 1:29.438 on lap 45.
